About me

I am a PhD candidate in Analytical Chemistry at the Laboratory of Analytical Chemistry, Department of Chemistry, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA), under the supervision of Prof. Nikolaos S. Thomaidis, and a member of the Trace Analysis and Mass Spectrometry (TrAMS) group.

I work at the interface of analytical and environmental chemistry, with a focus on mass spectrometry, MALDI imaging and chromatographic techniques. I approach method development through Analytical Quality by Design, building quality into sample preparation, instrumental methods and validation from the start.

Alongside my doctoral studies, I have delivered analytical services, contributed to national research projects and managed laboratory databases.

Research interests

  • Mass spectrometry (high and low resolution) and MALDI imaging
  • Analytical Quality by Design (AQbD) for sample preparation, method development and validation
  • LC-MS/MS and LC-TIMS-HRMS workflows
  • Target, suspect and non-target screening workflows
  • Determination of organic micropollutants and emerging contaminants in biological samples

In plain words

Every day, human-made chemicals, from pesticides and medicines to industrial additives, end up in the environment and eventually inside living organisms, often at levels far too low to see. I use mass spectrometry, a technique that “weighs” molecules with extreme precision, to find and measure these substances. With MALDI imaging I can go one step further and see where a chemical sits inside a tissue, almost like taking a chemical photograph.

Before any method is put to work, I design it so that quality is built in from the very start. This way, the numbers it produces are reliable enough to support decisions that protect both the environment and human health.

About me

Finding chemicals in biological samples and revealing their spatial distribution

Mass Spectrometry (High- and Low-resolution) · LC-TIMS-HRMS · LC-MS/MS · MALDI-MSI · MALDI-TIMS-QTOF MS · Target & suspect screening

Download CV Talks & posters Email me

I am a PhD candidate in Analytical Chemistry at the Laboratory of Analytical Chemistry, Department of Chemistry, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA), under the supervision of Prof. Nikolaos S. Thomaidis, and a member of the Trace Analysis and Mass Spectrometry (TrAMS) group.

I work at the interface of analytical and environmental chemistry, with a focus on mass spectrometry, MALDI mass spectrometry imaging (MALDI-MSI) and chromatographic techniques. I approach method development through Analytical Quality by Design, building quality into sample preparation, instrumental methods and validation from the start.

Within my doctoral research, I have carried out the analysis of small mammals (rodents and shrews, whole-body tissues) for organic micropollutants.

Research interests

  • High- and low-resolution mass spectrometry (HRMS, LC-MS/MS)
  • MALDI mass spectrometry imaging (MALDI-MSI) by MALDI-TIMS-QTOF MS, for the spatial distribution of chemicals in tissue
  • Analytical Quality by Design (AQbD) for sample preparation, method development and validation
  • LC-TIMS-HRMS workflows
  • Target, suspect and non-target screening workflows
  • Determination of organic micropollutants and emerging contaminants in biological samples
  • Complex biological matrices: small mammals (rodents and shrews, whole-body tissues)

Research projects

  • Project TerraChem (Horizon Europe): “From soils to apex species: chemical pathways, effects and impacts on terrestrial biodiversity and ecosystem services and applications for better chemicals management”.
  • Project MALDI4Health: application of MALDI-TIMS-QTOF MS methodologies to toxicity studies.
